### near-miss

Use case: illustration-story. Create a landscape 16:9 editorial illustration for a Singapore polytechnic staff workplace safety learning activity. Warm hand-painted flat shapes, delicate paper grain, muted navy, teal, cream and ochre, calm human-centred style, clean readable composition. In an office storage/copy area, a small cardboard archive box has JUST fallen from a shelf and landed on its side on the floor immediately beside an adult Asian colleague in a teal polo and dark trousers. The colleague stands clearly out of the box's path, surprised but entirely unhurt, looking down at it. A few sheets beside the box suggest the recent fall. Show clear separation between person and box: this is a near miss, not an injury. Shelf behind, one modest plant, campus office context. Medium-wide eye-level framing; box and colleague centrally composed and fully visible with crop-safe margins. No wounds, no dramatic danger, no broken property. No text, numbers, logos, UI, arrows, watermarks or decorative border. The image must clearly explain 'a falling box narrowly missed someone; nobody hurt'. High quality, web-ready illustration.

### chair-defect

Use case: illustration-story. Create a landscape 16:9 editorial illustration for a Singapore polytechnic staff workplace safety learning activity. Warm hand-painted flat shapes, delicate paper grain, muted navy, teal, cream and ochre, calm human-centred style, clean readable composition. A small campus meeting room with an EMPTY simple wooden four-legged chair in the foreground, angled three-quarter view. One front wooden leg has a clearly visible jagged crack, but is still attached; this is the unmistakable focal detail. A blank ochre tag hangs on the chair back, and a simple loose ochre barrier ribbon across the seat signals it must not be used. An adult Asian female staff colleague in modest teal office clothing stands safely beside the chair with a phone, noticing the defect and ready to request repair. Nobody sits on or touches the chair. Modest meeting table and plant in the background, uncluttered. Medium-wide eye-level framing; whole chair and colleague centrally composed, crop-safe margins, cracked leg fully visible. No injury, no accident, no repair work underway. No text, numbers, logos, UI, arrows, watermarks or decorative border. The image must clearly explain 'damaged chair kept out of use; request a repair'. High quality, web-ready illustration.
